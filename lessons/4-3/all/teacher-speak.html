4.3

Speaking practice — teacher’s script

TEACHER

← Страница Кати (Speaking practice) · номера ситуаций совпадают с кнопками на её странице

Как вести
  • Катя начинает разговор и идёт к своим трём пунктам. Ты сбиваешь каждые 20–40 секунд, реплики по порядку, можно пропускать.
  • Не сдавайся после первой же фразы: если она возвращает тему, через минуту сбей снова. Отпускай, только когда фраза прозвучала естественно и вежливо.
  • Если она просто игнорирует твою реплику или отвечает «ok» и продолжает — повтори громче: “Sorry, did you hear me?” Пусть отреагирует фразой.
  • Следи за трекером фраз на её странице: в следующей ситуации сбивай так, чтобы ей пришлось использовать то, что она ещё не говорила.
  • В конце каждой — 1 минута разбора: получила ли все 3 пункта, и как «ты» себя чувствовал(а) — happy / neutral / annoyed.

1Sprint planning

Ты — болтливый PM на sprint planning в Zoom. У Кати 5 минут до следующего созвона.

Цели Кати: Kate: who fixes the login bug and by when · who designs the onboarding screen · the date of the next demo.

🎭 Твой скрипт — сбивай по порядку, по одной реплике, когда она набирает темп
  1. Kate, hi! Before we start — how was your weekend? Did you go anywhere?
  2. Oh, that reminds me, did you see the new office plants? Somebody bought twenty of them!
  3. The login bug, yes, yes… By the way, do you think we should move to Linear instead of Jira?
  4. Wait, who’s going to the team dinner on Friday? We need a number.
  5. Speaking of design — I watched a great video about AI design tools yesterday…
  6. [смотришь в телефон] Sorry, one second… OK, what were we talking about?

Добиваемся фраз: We’ll come to that later · We’ll get to … in a moment · Anyway, as I was saying · Let’s get back to…

2Client call

Ты — клиент дизайн-студии. Отвечаешь на звонки и сообщения прямо во время созвона, как Mateo.

Цели Кати: Kate: approval for design version 2 · €3,000 for two extra weeks · next call booked.

🎭 Твой скрипт — сбивай по порядку, по одной реплике, когда она набирает темп
  1. Sorry, I have to take this… [10 сек по-итальянски / по-украински] OK, I’m back. Where were we?
  2. Version 2, yes. But first — what do you think about our competitor’s new website? I hate it.
  3. Oh, before I forget — can you also make the logo a bit bigger? And maybe blue?
  4. €3,000?! Hmm… Tell me, how many people work in your studio now?
  5. [читаешь вслух] ‘Meeting at 3’… Sorry, my calendar. So, what were you saying?
  6. Next call… Let’s talk about it next week. Or maybe by email. Or… what do you think about AI?

Добиваемся фраз: Sorry, can I just finish this point? · There will be plenty of time for that later · I really think we should get to…

31:1 with your manager

Ты — руководитель в хорошем настроении. Катя хочет €300 на курс и 2 часа в неделю на учёбу. Перед стартом она тянет style card (A — stay on track / B — go with the flow).

Цели Кати: Kate: €300 for a course · 2 hours a week of study time · when she gets an answer.

🎭 Твой скрипт — сбивай по порядку, по одной реплике, когда она набирает темп
  1. Kate! So nice to see you. How’s the team? Is everybody happy?
  2. You know, I’m planning my holiday — Greece or Portugal? What would you choose?
  3. The office move is a nightmare. Have you seen the new kitchen?
  4. A course? Great idea. And what do you think about the new junior? Is she OK?
  5. Two hours a week… Hmm. That reminds me, we need someone for the conference stand in March.
  6. Let me think about it. Oh, have you tried the new coffee place downstairs?

Добиваемся фраз: Option A: Is this a good moment to start talking about…? · I really think we should get to… · Option B: ждёт паузу и сама находит момент.

4Stakeholder update

Ты — Head of Sales. Катя — PM мобильного банкинга, у неё 10 минут на понедельничный апдейт.

Цели Кати: Kate: payment feature is 2 weeks late (and why) · one more QA tester for March · what Sales can promise clients this month.

🎭 Твой скрипт — сбивай по порядку, по одной реплике, когда она набирает темп
  1. Kate, quick question before you start — is it true that Mark is leaving?
  2. Two weeks late? Our competitor launched the same feature last month. Have you seen their ads?
  3. By the way, a client asked me if the app will have crypto. Will it?
  4. QA tester? That reminds me — HR is so slow, we still don’t have a new sales manager!
  5. I heard there will be a reorganisation in January. What do you know?
  6. Anyway, I promised the client the payment feature for next week. Is that OK?

Добиваемся фраз: We’ll get to … in a moment · We’ll come to that later · Can we move to …? · I really think we should get to…

5Freelancer call

Ты — дружелюбный фрилансер-разработчик. Лендинг нужен через 10 дней, а ты ещё ничего не прислал.

Цели Кати: Kate: what is ready / not ready · a new final date before the launch · daily Slack updates.

🎭 Твой скрипт — сбивай по порядку, по одной реплике, когда она набирает темп
  1. Hey Kate! Sorry, I’m a bit tired — I was at a hackathon all weekend. It was amazing!
  2. The landing page, yeah… I’m also working on a big project for a German bank, you know.
  3. Have I told you about my new hobby? I’m learning to surf! In November!
  4. What’s ready? Well, the design is in my head, haha. Do you like dark mode?
  5. Daily updates in Slack? Hmm, I prefer to just send everything at the end.
  6. Before I forget — can you pay the first invoice this week?

Добиваемся фраз: There will be plenty of time for that later · Let’s get back to… · Anyway, as I was saying · Sorry, can I just finish this point?

6Team retrospective

Ты — уставший член команды на ретро. Жалуешься на всё, кроме спринта.

Цели Кати: Kate: one thing that went well · one thing that went badly (about the sprint) · one action with an owner.

🎭 Твой скрипт — сбивай по порядку, по одной реплике, когда она набирает темп
  1. What went well? Nothing. The coffee machine is broken again.
  2. And the office is so cold. Why do we even go to the office?
  3. Honestly, the company doesn’t care about us. Did you see the email from the CEO?
  4. OK, the sprint… The designers were late. But that’s normal, they’re always late. My cat is always late too.
  5. Action? Let’s buy a new coffee machine! That’s my action.
  6. Who’s the owner? Not me. I’m on holiday next week, by the way — Bali!

Добиваемся фраз: We’ll come to that later · Let’s get back to the sprint · Can we move to …? · I really think we should get to…

7Hiring a junior PM

Ты — нервный кандидат на позицию junior PM. Говоришь слишком много и не по теме. У Кати 15 минут.

Цели Кати: Kate: one project the candidate managed · how they deal with a missed deadline · the next step.

🎭 Твой скрипт — сбивай по порядку, по одной реплике, когда она набирает темп
  1. Hi! Sorry, my internet is bad, I’m in a café. They have amazing croissants here.
  2. A project I managed… Well, at university I organised a party for 200 people. Also, I love travelling.
  3. Can I ask — do you work from home or from the office? And is there free lunch?
  4. A missed deadline… That reminds me, my previous boss was terrible. Let me tell you about him.
  5. What tools do you use? I love Notion. Have you seen my Notion templates? I can send them.
  6. Sorry, what was the question again?

Добиваемся фраз: We’ll get to that in a moment · There will be plenty of time for that later · Anyway, as I was saying · Before I forget (в конце — next step)

8Budget approval

Ты — финансовый директор, очень занят. Катя хочет AI-ассистента для заметок на митингах (€20/человек/месяц, команда 8 человек).

Цели Кати: Kate: the price · the benefit (≈3 hours saved per person per week) · yes / no / a date for the answer.

🎭 Твой скрипт — сбивай по порядку, по одной реплике, когда она набирает темп
  1. I have five minutes. Go. [сразу смотришь в телефон]
  2. Sorry — another call. [пауза 5 сек] OK. You were saying?
  3. AI tools… Everybody wants AI tools. Did you know Marketing asked for three last month?
  4. €20 per person… Can you also look at why our Zoom bill is so high?
  5. Before I forget — your team’s travel reports are late. Again.
  6. OK, send me an email. Or… what exactly do you want from me?

Добиваемся фраз: Can I just finish this point? · I really think we should get to… · Anyway, as I was saying · Is this a good moment to…?

9Kick-off with an Italian client

Ты — итальянский клиент (сеть отелей). Первый созвон, хочешь сначала познакомиться. Катя сама выбирает: Option A или B.

Цели Кати: Kate: the main goal of the project · the main contact on the client side · the next meeting.

🎭 Твой скрипт — сбивай по порядку, по одной реплике, когда она набирает темп
  1. Buongiorno! Where are you from? Have you ever been to Liguria?
  2. You must come to Italy. The food! Do you like pesto? Real pesto, not the supermarket one.
  3. The goal? Ah, we want… many things. But tell me, do you like football? Who do you support?
  4. Our main contact… Maybe me, maybe my cousin. It’s a family business, you know. Let me tell you about my family.
  5. Next meeting — after the holidays! In Italy we don’t work much in December, haha.
  6. That reminds me — we also have a restaurant. Maybe you can make an app for it too?

Добиваемся фраз: Option A: Can we move to …? · We’ll come to that later · Option B: слушает, задаёт вопросы, ловит момент и говорит Is this a good moment to start talking about…?

13Role swap — Катя меняет тему

Теперь у тебя повестка, а Катя тебя сбивает (минимум 4 раза, вежливо). Ты держишь тему.

Цели Кати: Твоя повестка (выбери одну): plan the Q1 roadmap · choose a new task tracker · prepare the client demo on Friday.

🎭 Твой скрипт — сбивай по порядку, по одной реплике, когда она набирает темп
  1. Ты начинаешь: OK, today we need to plan the Q1 roadmap. First, the big features.
  2. На её смену темы отвечай фразами staying on track — показываешь их со стороны.
  3. Если она застряла — подсказка: ‘Use your topics: Reddit, AI tool, conference, slow laptop, holiday.’
  4. После 2-х попыток можно ‘сдаться’: Ha, OK, tell me about that AI tool — чтобы она почувствовала, что сработало.

Добиваемся фраз: That reminds me · Before I forget · Is this a good moment to…? · Can we move to…?
